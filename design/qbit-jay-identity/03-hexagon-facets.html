Tradeoff

Most ownable and the strongest tie to the qbitdev family: the head is a hexagon, the acorn in the beak is the memory cache metaphor, and the single glow is the hexagon behind the bird. Facets are harder to draw by hand and give a less cuddly face than the other two; a flat-top hexagon tile (the qbitdev isotype is pointy-top, gradient stroke, orbit and Q) keeps it from reading as the qbitdev logo.

Type at implementation: Outfit 600, tracking −0.02em for the wordmark, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for labels; the artboard renders them with the system stack. Open question: whether a sub-brand of qbitdev should echo its hexagon this closely.
