Closest to the requested genre: a dark ground, one cyan rim glow (qbitdev glow-strong, one per piece), a saturated blue jay. Strongest in the README, TUI and dark web theme; the glow does not survive light backgrounds or print, so the light lockup moves the jay into a dark disc and the 32 px head drops the glow for a plain cyan rim.

Type at implementation: Outfit 600, tracking −0.02em for the wordmark (qbitdev wordmark rule: lowercase, never split), Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for labels. The artboard renders them with the system stack.
