Tradeoff

Friendliest and most flexible: thick ink outline and a white die-cut border make it a sticker that holds on light, dark or photo backgrounds, in print, and at 32 px. Gives up the glow genre of the request; the neon feeling survives only through the cyan isotype disc and qbitdev colour.

Type at implementation: Outfit 700 for the wordmark with −0.02em tracking (weight is heavier to match the outline), Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for labels. The artboard renders them with the system stack.
